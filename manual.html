Driftwaver — User Manual

For version 1.5 · Last updated 13 October 2026

Driftwaver plays and generates ambient audio with binaural tones — two slightly different frequencies, one per ear, which is why the app keeps mentioning headphones. Without stereo headphones the music still plays, but the effect the app is built around does not happen.

The three tabs

Everything lives under three tabs along the bottom.

TabWhat it is for
PlayListening. The player, the queue, the binaural switch, the sleep timer, the alarm, and the animated background.
CreateMaking a new track — either on your phone for free, or with AI in the cloud.
My Media
(My Media-tation)		Your collection: the built-in library, tracks you have added, and sessions you have played.

Play — the player

Now playing, and the queue

The card at the top shows what is playing and the queue beneath it. Tap any row to switch to it. If something is already playing and has not been exported, the app asks before replacing it, and offers Export first — that prompt exists because a mix you have not saved is gone once you load something else.

The + button adds a track: from the built-in Library, from your Exported tracks, or as a File from device. The ⋮ on each row removes that row or clears the list. Clearing the list deletes nothing — every track stays in My Media-tation, and choosing it there brings it back.

The controls row

ControlWhat it does
Sound settings (gear with a note)Opens the binaural settings — frequencies, volumes, pink noise. See Sound settings.
Play / PauseThe round button in the middle.
HeadphonesThe binaural switch. It has four states — see below.

The binaural switch and its four states

This is the control most worth understanding, because it looks different depending on what the track already has.

AppearanceMeaningWhat tapping does
Grey headphones, no labelTones are off. The music plays plain.Turns tones on.
Gold headphones with arcs, “Binaural on”Tones are playing over the music.Turns them off.
Faded, “Tap to generate”You added this file yourself and nothing has been computed for it yet.Asks first, then generates. Takes about half a minute and restarts the track.
Locked, “Already in this track”The tones are already inside the audio — usually an export you re-imported.Nothing. Adding a second set would beat against the first.

When the switch says Tap to generate, Export mix is greyed out too. That is deliberate: until the waves exist there is no mix to export, and exporting anyway would produce a file with a flat tone that ignores the music. Generating re-opens both.

Full screen

The animated space below the player is not decoration you are stuck with.

  • Swipe in the upper area to change the drifting objects.
  • Swipe in the lower area to change the background.
  • The four-arrows button at the lower right opens full screen.

In full screen the animation fills the display with nothing on top of it. Tap once and the controls fade in: previous track, play/pause, next track, and the exit button at the lower right. They fade out again on their own, so the view returns to being just the animation. The two swipe areas work here as well.

Sleep timer and alarm clock

Sleep timer

The clock-and-gear icon. Choose 5, 10, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes and playback stops when it runs out. It reads Off when nothing is set, and shows the remaining time while it counts down.

Alarm clock

The alarm icon opens a full screen with four parts:

  • Hour and Minute wheels.
  • Repeat — pick days of the week. Pick none and it rings once, then turns itself off.
  • Wake to — choose a track. Choose nothing and the alarm follows whatever is loaded.
  • Wake-up binaural — a rising sweep from delta to alpha, 2 to 10 Hz, intended to bring you up gently rather than jolt you.

Android requires its own permission for exact alarms. The app asks once; without it an alarm would be saved but never ring.

Sound settings

Reached from the gear on the Play tab, or from the ⋮ menu.

SettingWhat it means
Entrainment goalA preset such as Fall Asleep: Theta → Delta (6 → 2 Hz). This is the beat frequency and the direction it moves in over the session. Direction underneath restates it in plain numbers.
Save current as presetKeeps your own combination under a name.
Tone volume (start / end)How loud the binaural tone is at the beginning and at the end. Two values, because the session moves between them.
Music volume (start / end)The same for the music underneath.
Pink noiseAdds a soft wash of noise.
Show advanced settingsCarrier frequency, semitone jump threshold, ramp smoothing and similar. Safe to ignore.

Create — making music

Two methods, chosen by the toggle at the top.

Local synthesis — free, offline, unlimited

Runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere and it costs nothing. Two modes:

  • Composer — pick a Preset (Ethereal, Deep Sleep, Warm Ambient and so on), optionally write a Prompt describing the music in words, set a Duration, and press Generate. New Variation produces a different piece from the same description.
  • Sliders — the same engine with the musical parameters exposed directly.

The app estimates how long it will take on your phone, and learns from how long previous renders actually took. Replaying a session you have made before is immediate — it is kept, not rebuilt.

AI generation — Pro, uses the cloud

Sends your description to a cloud service that runs the music models. Choose a voice — AI-Aster, AI-Elmi or AI-Lika — write a description, set a duration, and press Generate Music. The line underneath shows what is left of your monthly allowance, for example 99 tracks or 117 min left this period.

Options shared by both

  • Cover with binaural tones — on, the track is made with tones already mixed in; off, it is a plain meditation track.
  • Track name — names the saved file and the session in the Play tab. Left blank, the description or preset is used.
  • Save prompt as… / Saved prompts — keep a description you like and reuse it.

Generated files are saved to Downloads/Driftwaver on your phone.

My Media-tation — library and sessions

Library

The built-in catalogue. Each track shows its style and length, and whether it is on your phone:

  • ✓ Included offline — shipped with the app, plays immediately.
  • ☁ Cloud · 9.1 MB — available to Download first.

Sessions

What you have played, newest first, each labelled with where it came from — Library, From device — and when. This is also where files you add from your phone live.

Exporting a mix

Export mix appears on the Play tab once a track is loaded. It renders the music and the binaural tones together into one .m4a file you can keep, share or use elsewhere, with your current sound settings baked in. A different entrainment goal gives a genuinely different file, so exporting the same track twice is a reasonable thing to do.

The button is greyed out when there is no mix to make: a track that already contains tones, or one whose waves have not been generated yet.

Free use and Driftwaver Pro

Most of the app is free and stays free: the whole library, binaural tones, local synthesis, your own files, the alarm and the sleep timer.

Free use includes a limited number of exports and of binaural analyses for your own tracks, and a selection of the animated objects and backgrounds. Driftwaver Pro is a monthly subscription that adds AI generation — up to 120 minutes and 100 generations a month — and the rest of the artwork. It renews automatically until cancelled, and you cancel in Google Play.

Restore subscription on the Pro screen recovers an existing subscription on a new phone or after reinstalling.

The ★ and ⋮ buttons

  • ★ opens Driftwaver Pro.
  • ⋮ holds Sound settings, Driftwaver Pro, About app and Language. The app is available in English, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, German, French, Spanish and Polish.

Safety

If you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder or a pacemaker, or you are pregnant, speak to a doctor before using rhythmic audio. Do not listen while driving or operating machinery. Stop if you feel unwell.
