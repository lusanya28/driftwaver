# Driftwaver Music — legal pages

Hosts the two documents Google Play requires at a stable HTTPS URL for
[Driftwaver Music](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.driftwaver.music)
(`com.driftwaver.music`), an Android app for binaural tones, ambient music and AI-generated
tracks.

- [Privacy Policy](https://lusanya28.github.io/driftwaver/privacy-policy.html)
- [Terms of Use](https://lusanya28.github.io/driftwaver/terms-of-use.html)

Served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch. **These URLs must not change**: they are recorded
in the Play Console listing and compiled into the app, so a rename or a moved file breaks both
and needs a new app release to repair.

Corrections and content reports: magic4pay@gmail.com

---

No licence, deliberately. These are not reusable templates — they are the terms that bind users
of one specific app, and a permissive licence would invite modified copies to circulate as if
they came from us. Read them; do not fork them.
