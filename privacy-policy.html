Driftwaver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Driftwaver is a meditation and focus audio app for Android (com.driftwaver.music). This policy describes what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does very little with it.

What we do not collect

Driftwaver has no user accounts and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software of any kind. It does not read your device identifier, advertising ID, location, contacts, calendar, photos, call logs or messages, and it has no permission to do so. We do not build a profile of you and we have nothing to sell.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved presets, saved prompts, and the list of tracks you have added or downloaded are stored locally on your device only. Generated and downloaded audio files are saved on your device (generated tracks go to Downloads/Driftwaver). None of this is sent to us.

All of this travels over HTTPS.

About the AI prompt text

This is the one piece of content you author that leaves your device, so it deserves to be spelled out. The description you write leaves our systems and is processed on a third-party cloud service (RunPod), where the audio models run, and it may appear in that service's operational logs. Please do not put personal, confidential or identifying information into it — it is a description of music, and nothing else needs to be there.

We do not store your prompts on our servers beyond what is needed to pass the request through and return the audio; the text itself is not kept. The audio generated from it is kept — see below.

What we keep, and for how long

The audio of each AI track you generate Our storage (Cloudflare R2) 7 days, then deleted automatically (the sweep runs on daily boundaries, so removal can fall a few hours later). Nothing here needs a request from you to disappear: the audio expires on its own, and the tally resets with the period. If you would like a generated track removed sooner, write to us with its generation id — the same id the Report content button includes — and we will delete it.

AI-generated content

Tracks produced by the AI feature are generated by machine-learning models from your description. Output can be unpredictable. If a generated track is offensive or otherwise objectionable, please report it using the Report content button shown beside a finished track, or write to the address below. Include the generation ID shown in the report so we can trace it.

Children

Driftwaver is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Almost everything the app stores about you is on your device. Uninstalling Driftwaver removes it, except for audio files you chose to save to your Downloads folder, which remain yours to keep or delete.

There is no account and no profile: we hold no name, address, email or device identifier, so there is nothing of that kind to export or erase. What we do hold is listed above — generated audio, which deletes itself after 7 days, and a usage tally against a random identifier, which resets each period. Write to us if you want a generated track removed sooner; uninstalling the app abandons the random identifier, and with it the tally.
